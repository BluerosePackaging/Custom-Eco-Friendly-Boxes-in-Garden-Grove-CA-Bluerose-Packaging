The best packaging choice depends on what is being packed and how the product will move through the supply chain. A lightweight apparel item has different requirements from a glass container, electronic device, or heavy industrial component.

- **Weight and Fragility:** Select a box construction that can accommodate the contents and expected handling conditions.
- **Material Composition:** Ask about recycled content, recyclability, and other relevant material attributes.

## Eco-Friendly Packaging for Shipping and Fulfillment

Packaging must remain practical throughout storage, order preparation, transportation, and delivery. A recyclable box that does not provide sufficient protection for its contents may lead to product damage and additional waste.

Businesses can improve their packaging decisions by reviewing the following points:

- Choose box dimensions that accommodate the product without excessive unused space.
- Use suitable protective inserts for fragile or irregularly shaped products.
- Consider the product's weight when selecting corrugated construction.
- Avoid unnecessary packaging layers when they do not improve protection.
- Review how boxes will be stored and handled before dispatch.
- Make sure closure methods and additional packaging materials suit the shipping application.
- Confirm that any environmental claims accurately describe the materials used.

For products that require specialized protection, packaging performance should remain a priority alongside material sustainability.
